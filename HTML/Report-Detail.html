Facility Report: Clean Water Outage in School Dormitory

By David | 2 Hours ago • Location: 2nd Floor Student Dormitory

Assalamualaikum Warahmatullahi Wabarakatuh. Through this report, we would like to inform that the clean water supply in the ablution area and all bathrooms of the 2nd-floor student dormitory building has stopped completely since 05:30 AM this morning. Water taps in all cubicles are not dispensing any water, disrupting morning congregational prayers and routine student hygiene activities.

Upon preliminary inspection by student representatives, the main automatic water pump feeding the rooftop tank did not activate automatically. This caused the roof tank to deplete faster than usual during the morning routine rush.

"Clean water supply is an essential facility for the smooth conduct of student worship, dormitory cleanliness, and mutual health. We earnestly request the School Facility Team to conduct prompt inspection and repair."

As a result of this outage, many students were forced to queue at the ground floor restrooms and the school mosque ablution station, causing several students to arrive slightly late for the first morning class.

We kindly request the Facility & Infrastructure (Sarpras) division of SMK IDN Boarding School to inspect the water tank sensor valve and pump automatic switchboard immediately. Should replacement parts require extended time, we appreciate guidance or emergency water provisioning for 2nd-floor students. Thank you for your swift attention and response.
