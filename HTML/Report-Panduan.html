Let's Start (Create Report)
Practical Workflow

4 Steps to Report Issues

From discovering a problem in the dorm or classroom to technician resolution.

Step 1
Open the Form

Click the "Let's Start (Create Report)" button available on the navbar or the All Reports page.

Step 2
Fill Issue Details

Enter the issue title, choose category (Facility, Lab/IT, Dormitory), and specify the room or floor location.

Step 3
Choose Privacy Option

You can provide your name & class or toggle the Anonymous option for privacy.

Step 4
Track Progress Status

Submit your report, then monitor technician progress updates directly on the All Reports page.

Report Status Meanings

Every submitted report is tagged with the following status badge:

Pending Report Received

The report has been recorded and is awaiting review from the Facility Team administrator.

In Progress Under Investigation

A technician or designated staff member is actively inspecting or repairing the issue.

Completed Fully Resolved

The issue has been resolved and the facility is back to normal operation.

Reporting Guidelines & Ethics

Important principles to observe when submitting reports:

  • Provide Specific Location: State the exact floor, room number, or lab desk so technicians can locate the issue quickly.
  • Polite & Respectful Language: Describe concerns objectively, courteously, and clearly without bias.
  • No Pranks or False Reports: False reports hinder others who genuinely require urgent facility repairs.
  • Avoid Spam Submissions: No need to submit duplicate reports for the same issue once it is marked In Progress.
