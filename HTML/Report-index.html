About IDN E-Report

IDN E-Report is an integrated complaint system developed by 10th grade Software Engineering (RPL B) students of SMK IDN Boarding School. This platform directly connects students with School Facilities, Computer & Network Labs, and Dormitory Supervisors to ensure every issue is resolved promptly and transparently.
